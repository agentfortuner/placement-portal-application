Placement Code of Conduct

Official Guidelines for the Academic Year 2025-26

1 Eligibility Criteria

Students must maintain the minimum CGPA specified by the visiting company. Any student with active backlogs at the time of the drive will be automatically disqualified unless specified otherwise by the HR.

2 Attendance & Punctuality

Participation in Pre-Placement Talks (PPT) is mandatory. Failure to attend a registered drive without 24-hour prior notice will lead to a Temporary Deactivation of the portal account.

3 Disciplinary Action

Misconduct during interviews, falsifying resume data, or direct communication with company HRs outside the portal is strictly prohibited and may lead to a permanent ban.

4 Layered Placement Policy (IIT Model)

To ensure equitable distribution of opportunities, the following CTC-based rules apply to all students:

Category CTC Range (LPA) Policy Status
Gold Package Above 15.0 LPA Final. Student is barred from all further drives.
Silver Package 7.0 - 15.0 LPA Restricted. Can only sit for one "Gold" attempt.
Base Package Below 7.0 LPA Active. Can sit for "Silver" or "Gold" categories.
Important Note on "Waitlists"

If a student is waitlisted by a "Gold" company, they may continue to sit for other drives until the offer is confirmed. Once confirmed, all other pending applications will be automatically withdrawn by the Admin.

By proceeding with registration, you are legally bound by these institutional guidelines.
