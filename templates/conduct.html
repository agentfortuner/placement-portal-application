Placement Code of Conduct

Guidelines for the Placement Season 2025-26

1. Eligibility

Students must maintain the minimum CGPA specified by the visiting company. Students with active backlogs during the placement drive may be disqualified unless stated otherwise by the company.

2. Attendance

Attendance for Pre-Placement Talks (PPT) is mandatory. Missing a registered drive without prior notice may lead to temporary suspension from the placement portal.

3. Disciplinary Action

Misconduct during interviews, falsifying resume information, or contacting company HR outside the portal is strictly prohibited and may result in permanent removal from the placement process.

4. Placement Offer Policy
Category CTC Range Policy
Gold Package Above 15 LPA Final offer. Student cannot participate in further drives.
Silver Package 7 – 15 LPA Student may attempt one Gold company.
Base Package Below 7 LPA Student may continue applying for Silver or Gold companies.
Startup Offer Variable / Equity Based Students accepting startup offers may still apply to higher packages depending on institute policy and admin approval.
Note: If a student receives a confirmed offer, all other active applications will be withdrawn by the placement office.
