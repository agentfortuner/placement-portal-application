Placement Code of Conduct

Guidelines for Placement Season 2025-26

Eligibility

All students graduating in 2026 are eligible to participate in the placement process provided they meet the academic criteria specified by the respective companies.

Attendance

Students who register for a company's placement drive must attend the pre-placement talk (PPT) and all subsequent rounds. Failure to attend without prior valid intimation will lead to disqualification.

Disciplinary

Any form of malpractice, falsification of data in resumes, or misbehavior during interviews will result in strict disciplinary action, including debarment from the placement process.

Offer Policy

The placement portal follows a strict offer policy based on the company tier. Please refer to the table below:

Current Offer Tier Can apply to Gold? Can apply to Silver? Can apply to Base? Can apply to Startup?
None Yes Yes Yes Yes
Startup Yes Yes Yes No
Base / Silver Yes No No No
Gold No No No No
Note: Once a student receives a Gold tier offer, they will be deregistered from the placement process.
